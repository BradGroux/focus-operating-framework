# Focus Operating Framework v1.1.0

- **Release date:** 2026-08-22
- **Type:** Minor, documentation-only
- **Previous release:** [v1.0.0](https://github.com/BradGroux/focus-operating-framework/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
- **Specification:** [v1.1.0](../specifications/v1.1.0.md)

## Summary

Version 1.1.0 adopts Open Framework Commons v1.1.0, whose explicit ecosystem
decision recognizes Focus Operating Framework as the fifth independent product.
It also adds bounded contributor templates and makes the exact-candidate review
and release instructions reusable after the initial release.

## Commons adoption

Focus adopts the Commons v1.1.0 annotated tag at peeled commit
`f25a2b89b4aed95984fd235e2e229efe52c125d8`. The compatibility review found no
change to shared principles or research-and-review expectations and no conflict
with Focus doctrine. [ADR 0009](../../docs/adr/0009-adopt-open-framework-commons-v1-1-0.md)
records the complete local decision.

Recognition does not transfer authority. Focus remains independently governed
and retains control of its purpose, audience, method, terminology, evidence,
releases, licensing, and implementations.

## Repository stewardship

- Added a structured Focus change issue form and pull-request template.
- Added a machine-readable `VERSION` file.
- Generalized release governance and reusable review prompts for later exact
  versions.
- Preserved the documentation-only boundary by adding no application code,
  automation, CI, runtime, or conformance machinery.

## Framework compatibility

The release does not change the Focus definition, Mission, first job,
commitments, concerns, moves, authority model, one-individual scope, paper path,
selective admission, Journal privacy, AI boundary, optional Personal Mission
Control pattern, portable exit, or evidence limits.

Focus v1.0.0, including its Commons v1.0.0 pin, review records, annotated tag,
and release metadata, remains immutable.

## Verification boundary

The verification required by the [v1.1.0 specification](../specifications/v1.1.0.md)
establishes the identity, internal consistency, and publication integrity of
the documentation release. It does not prove improved focus, output, wellbeing,
safety, compliance, or general effectiveness.
