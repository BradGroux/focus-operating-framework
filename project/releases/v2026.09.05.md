# Focus Operating Framework v2026.09.05

- **Edition date:** 2026-09-05 (UTC publication date)
- **Predecessor:** [v1.1.0](https://github.com/BradGroux/focus-operating-framework/releases/tag/v1.1.0)
- **Scope:** Documentation; substantive clarification and reduced recordkeeping

## What changes for the individual

Recovery can begin without a cue or a workable time block. Missed routines do
not create catch-up debt. Rest, no new record and abandoning a private plan are
valid choices. Existing destination evidence can serve as an Action Receipt;
a second manual log is unnecessary when request, result and readback remain
inspectable. Releasing an intention does not cancel an external commitment or
authorize deleting someone else's records. Unknown outcomes remain unknown.

## Compatibility and adoption

This edition relaxes duplicate recordkeeping and makes minimum practice explicit.
Readers or implementations requiring full cue templates, catch-up, uninterrupted
work or duplicate receipts should reconsider those requirements. The ten
commitments, six concerns, seven moves, individual authority, paper path,
selective admission, private Journal and optional derived Personal Mission
Control remain. AI is still optional, source-backed and unable to execute an
external mutation, including after confirmation.

at peeled commit `8868a248457dd7b663563beb243c5ebcbb8ac360`, without deviations
