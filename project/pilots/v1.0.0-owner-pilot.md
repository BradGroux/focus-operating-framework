## Purpose

Test whether Focus helps the founding steward preserve continuity and direct
attention across representative work, personal, and combined contexts without
creating disproportionate review burden, authority confusion, privacy failure,
coercion, or tool dependence.

The pilot does not test a Focus application and does not support a claim of
general effectiveness.

## Episode measures

For each episode, record:

- life scope: work, personal, or combined;
- path: paper-only, digital-only, physical-digital, unassisted, AI-assisted, or
  a truthful combination;
- moves used;
- whether restart effort was lower, unchanged, higher, or not applicable;
- usefulness from 1 to 5;
- net burden: whether review and correction burden was lower than, equal to, or
  higher than the time, confusion, or risk removed;
- whether privacy friction occurred;
- whether authority confusion occurred and was resolved;
- whether applicable provenance and authority were recoverable;
- whether any material privacy, accidental-sharing, silent-overwrite, or
  unauthorized-action failure occurred; and
- a sanitized observation with no private subject matter.
