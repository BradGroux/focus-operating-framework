## Explicit checks that passed with no finding

- The package gives one individual usable entry points, questions, minimum results, examples, and stop conditions without creating a mandatory sequence.
- Purpose, selected life scope, assumptions, limits, privacy boundaries, and authority can be identified before action.
- Selective admission remains possible on physical, digital, Journal, Mission Control, and mixed-surface paths without capture-everything pressure.
- Source, derivative, corrected derivative, proposal, accepted record, projection, record of authority, freshness, conflict, and provenance remain distinguishable wherever applicable.
- Paper-only and digital-only practice are both complete; paper is neither required nor subordinated to digitization.
- Unassisted practice is complete and is never described as a fallback.
- Optional AI assistance is purpose-bounded, limited to admitted material, source-backed, proposal-only, and subject to human review.
- AI is separated from every external mutation. Exact human control, destination preview, consequential-detail review, destination readback, and Action Receipts are explicit.
- Private Check-Ins, Reflections, Journal material, proposals, and decision history do not become shared merely because the subject is work-related.
- Combined life scope does not merge records or authorize team access, whole-life capture, or a universal source of truth.
- Stale projections and conflicts remain visible; silent merge, silent overwrite, and latest-wins behavior are rejected.
- Portable exit requires understandable, usable context and a viable manual path rather than an opaque dump.
- Deferral, release, dismissal, deletion, no action, and stopping are valid outcomes.
- The seven examples are explicitly fictional and non-authoritative and make no general effectiveness claim.

- A `GO` does not establish improved focus, output, wellbeing, safety, compliance, or general effectiveness.
