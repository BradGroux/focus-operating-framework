## Navigation and structure results

- The README supplies a clear starting point, a seven-document reading order,
  a one-minute minimum practice, a repository map, and direct links to canonical
  language, examples, release evidence, governance, contribution, license, and
  citation surfaces.
- Heading levels are correctly nested. Headings are descriptive and sufficiently
  distinct for navigation within each document.
- All checked local Markdown links and heading targets resolve. Link labels are
  understandable in their lists, metadata, and surrounding navigation surfaces.
- Long documents use descriptive sections, short lists, bounded tables, and
  repeated stopping guidance instead of hiding requirements in uninterrupted
  prose.
- Tables have headers and consistent columns. Their surrounding prose supplies
  the governing meaning. The wide owner-pilot episode table has an explicitly
  equivalent linear episode form for plain-text and assistive reading surfaces.
- Both Mermaid diagrams have complete prose alternatives. The physical-digital
  relationship is explained by the following numbered sections, and the Mission
  Control relationship is explained by the surrounding composition, authority,
  review, action, and exit sections.
- Repository names and paths support predictable discovery, and the framework
  remains usable as plain Markdown without rendering extensions or a companion
  application.

## Plain-language results

- The README defines derivative, record of authority, and promotion before the
  one-minute practice uses them. The canonical language reference defines the
  remaining specialized terms, and practical documents restate the distinctions
  near use.
- Sentences consistently identify the individual as the decision and action
  authority. AI, connector, proposal, acceptance, promotion, and external-action
  language names who may decide, confirm, invoke, change, send, or delete.
- Sources, derivatives, proposals, accepted records, projections, corrections,
  and records of authority remain distinct in prose, tables, and examples.
- Candidate, planned, incomplete, accepted, and released states are not
  conflated. The owner pilot remains explicitly not started, and release files
  distinguish content identity from publication proof.
- Exclusions explain practical boundaries such as no whole-life capture, no team
  workspace, no ambient recording, no silent overwrite, and no autonomous
  external mutation.
- A reader can perform a minimum Focus episode from the README or practice guide
  without reading the full package.

## Cognitive-burden and non-coercion results

- The six concerns are presented as subjects to keep clear, while the seven
  moves are optional actions. They are never combined into thirteen required
  steps, and the numbered one-minute presentation immediately states that the
  moves are not a sequence.
- Checklists, matrices, contracts, and tables are presented as optional or
  situation-specific aids. Completion does not require every concern, move,
  surface, record, or supporting pattern.
- Defer, release, stop, dismissal, no action, responsible restraint, and smaller
  practice are repeatedly available as successful outcomes.
- Review Items require source, freshness, why-now rationale, choices, authority,
  and boundaries. Focus Reviews are individually chosen, bounded, dismissible,
  and free of zero-inbox or fixed-cadence requirements.
- Transition Check-Ins, Reflection, Journal, AI, paper, digital tools, Personal
  Mission Control, and connectors remain optional. Paper-only, digital-only,
  unassisted, and AI-assisted paths remain valid as specified.
- No streak, shame, hidden ranking, coercive urgency, constant-vigilance, or
  mandatory-completion pattern is introduced.
- The owner-pilot form uses sanitized measures, offers a linear alternative to
  its wide table, makes pilot completion non-prerequisite evidence work, and
  directs the owner to stop or reshape rather than complete at any cost. It does
  not require public disclosure of private episode content or an external
  mutation.

## Inclusive-scope and accessibility results

- The framework assumes one choosing individual but no particular job, family
  structure, physical ability, communication method, provider access, or
  cognitive style.
- Paper is first-class but not mandatory. Digital tools, AI, connectors, and
  Personal Mission Control are optional and provider-neutral.
- A deliberate spoken source is one optional capture surface. Voice and ambient
  recording are expressly not required.
- Color, position, and arrows do not carry the only explanation of any
  distinction. Diagram meaning is repeated in prose, and tables remain readable
  row by row.
- Time and item bounds are chosen by the individual. Specific durations in
  fictional examples illustrate local choices rather than universal limits.
- Medical, therapeutic, crisis, diagnostic, legal, employment, and
  wellbeing-treatment guidance and guarantees remain outside scope.
- All seven examples include fictional and non-authoritative status, purpose,
  life scope, assumptions, limits, concerns, moves, provenance and authority,
  boundaries, an illustrative outcome, and non-generalized lessons.
- Each reusable review prompt identifies exact inputs, explicit preflight and
  stop conditions, permitted evidence, a bounded role, and a defined output
  structure. Verification and disposition prompts distinguish their non-review
  verdict labels from independent-review verdicts.
