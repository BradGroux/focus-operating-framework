## Navigation and structure results

- `README.md` gives a clear seven-document reading order, points separately to
  canonical language and examples, and provides a self-contained one-minute
  entry point before release and contributor material.
- The one-minute practice defines the three authority terms needed by its
  questions, states that the moves are non-sequential and optional, and includes
  a small Transition Check-In that can complete a minimum episode.
- Heading levels are correctly nested. Headings are descriptive and produce no
  ambiguous targets within a document.
- Candidate-relative Markdown links and heading fragments resolve with matching
  filename case. Link labels identify their destinations without relying on
  labels such as "here" or "this link."
- Tables have headers and consistent columns. Their surrounding prose states the
  governing meaning. The wide owner-pilot episode table has an equivalent linear
  form, so the table is not the only usable entry surface.
- The physical-digital and Personal Mission Control diagrams are accompanied by
  prose that communicates their essential distinctions. The physical-digital
  text says the arrows are illustrative and skippable; the Mission Control text
  explains that inputs remain authoritative elsewhere and the view is bounded.
- The repository map, canonical filenames, examples index, pilot index, review
  index, and prompt inventory support predictable discovery. The framework
  remains understandable in plain text without diagram rendering or a companion
  application.

## Plain-language results

- Canonical terms are defined in `CONTEXT.md` and are introduced near first
  practical use in the README and framework guides. The one-minute practice does
  not require a reader to consult the complete terminology reference first.
- Sentences consistently identify the individual as the decision-maker for
  admission, correction, acceptance, promotion, attention, and action. AI,
  connectors, records, and derived views are not given vague implied authority.
- Source, derivative, proposal, accepted record, projection, promotion,
  permission, approval, and external mutation remain verbally distinct in the
  representation and connector tables and in surrounding prose.
- Status language distinguishes accepted framework documents, fictional
  examples, the planned and not-started owner pilot, clean candidates, generated
  review evidence, verified final candidates, and published releases.
- Exclusions state practical boundaries, including what may not be captured,
  shared, inferred, overwritten, automated, or treated as evidence.
- All seven examples identify status, purpose, life scope, assumptions, limits,
  concerns, moves, provenance and authority, boundaries, illustrative outcome,
  and lessons. Their outcomes distinguish observed fictional events from choices
  and explicitly reject effectiveness claims.

## Cognitive-burden and non-coercion results

- The six concerns are persistent subjects of judgment and the seven moves are
  optional actions, not thirteen steps. Readers may enter anywhere, skip or
  repeat moves, use one surface, or stop.
- Minimum-result lists, tables, and checklists are bounded aids. Completion does
  not require all concerns, all moves, a durable record, a digital artifact, a
  Journal Entry, AI, Mission Control, a connector, or external action.
- Defer, release, dismissal, leave unchanged, stop, and no action are repeated as
  legitimate outcomes across the quick start, practice guide, reviews, examples,
  and stop conditions.
- Review Items require source, freshness, why-now rationale, choices, authority,
  and boundaries. Focus Reviews are individually chosen, bounded, dismissible,
  and free of zero-inbox, fixed-cadence, repeated-nagging, hidden-priority,
  streak, and shame requirements.
- Transition Check-Ins, Reflection, Journal, AI assistance, paper, digital tools,
  Personal Mission Control, connectors, and resurfacing remain optional. Paper-
  only, digital-only, and unassisted paths remain complete.
- Safety and misuse warnings are direct and give a stopping, narrowing,
  disconnection, deletion, or manual-continuity response without requiring
  constant vigilance.
- The owner-pilot materials prohibit fabricated or private episode content,
  provide a sanitized evidence boundary, state that no external mutation is
  required, treat incomplete or stopped status as valid, and direct the owner to
  stop or reshape rather than complete the target at any cost.

## Inclusive-scope and accessibility results

- Focus is scoped to one individual without assuming a job type, employer,
  family structure, physical ability, communication method, cognitive style, or
  complete access to any tool.
- Paper is first-class but optional; digital-only practice is complete. Digital
  tools and AI are optional, replaceable, provider-neutral, and paired with
  ordinary-file or manual continuation.
- Spoken capture is deliberate, foreground, and optional. Ambient recording and
  passive surveillance are excluded.
- No distinction depends only on color, spatial position, or an arrow. Status and
  authority are named in prose and table cells.
- Example durations and review bounds are presented as local choices or
  illustrative conditions. The bounded Focus Review can use time, item count,
  context, or decision type, and ends when its bound is reached or the individual
  stops.
- Medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, crisis, and wellbeing-treatment guidance and
  guarantees are explicitly outside scope. Journal AI guidance also prohibits
  diagnosis and medical or therapeutic claims.
- Each reusable prompt names its inputs, evidence boundary, preflight and stop
  conditions, role, required checks, output structure, and invalid-input verdict.
  Verification prompts distinguish read-only verification from independent
  review and prohibit corrective mutation.

## Limitations

This was a documentation review, not a formal accessibility certification, user
study, cognitive-load study, or assistive-technology conformance test. It did not
evaluate a software implementation because the release defines none. The
examples are fictional and the owner pilot is not started, so the review supports
only clarity, navigability, non-coercion, and documented applicability for the
exact candidate, not real-world effectiveness or suitability for every reader.
