## Authority-order and Commons-boundary results

The public authority order is consistent in `AGENTS.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, the
v1.0.0 specification, and the charter's amendment boundary:

1. direct current owner instruction for active repository work;
2. `framework/charter.md`;
3. accepted ADRs under `docs/adr/`;
4. canonical framework documents and `CONTEXT.md`;
5. governance and repository policies; and
6. examples, pilot records, review records, and project history.

Lower-authority surfaces are explicitly prevented from silently redefining
higher-authority material. Examples remain illustrative, pilot records remain
bounded practice evidence, reviews remain exact-target findings, and
implementations remain non-authoritative. No repeated practice, pilot result,
review report, implementation, or Commons change receives implicit amendment
authority.

Every current-adoption Focus surface uses Commons v1.1.0 and peeled commit
`f25a2b89b4aed95984fd235e2e229efe52c125d8`. The exact Commons tag is annotated
and resolves to that commit. Historical v1.0.0 specifications, changelog text,
and review records retain their earlier v1.0.0 pin and are clearly scoped to
that immutable release.

Focus adopts Commons by exact reference while retaining its local purpose,
audience, method, terminology, examples, evidence, licensing, governance,
release authority, and implementation choices. Commons v1.1.0 recognizes Focus
as an independent product with equal provenance and importance; neither the
Commons decision nor Focus's adoption is described as inheritance or
transferred authority. Each repository requires its own later decision before
the other's changes have local effect. No Commons method was copied into Focus
as parent doctrine, and no conflict with the tagged Commons principles or
boundaries was found.
