## Plain-language results

The canonical language reference defines specialized terms and supplies avoid-language that prevents common collapses. The README places the language reference beside the reading order and locally defines derivative, record of authority, and promotion before the one-minute practice uses them. Practical documents consistently identify the individual as the decision-maker and distinguish source facts, derivatives, proposals, accepted records, projections, promotions, and external actions.

Authority language is active and accountable. The individual designates records of authority, admits material, reviews and accepts or rejects proposals, approves promotions, chooses attention, and performs or separately invokes external action. Permission is repeatedly distinguished from approval, and AI output never receives authority through fluency, silence, access, or prior approval.

Status language remains distinct: the charter is accepted for v1.0.0; examples are fictional, illustrative, and non-authoritative; the owner pilot is planned and not started; reusable prompts contain unresolved placeholders by design; and the content baseline does not claim that a working copy proves publication.

The exclusions explain practical consequences rather than relying only on labels. Team scope, whole-life capture, ambient collection, Journal exposure, provider dependence, AI authority, medical or therapeutic use, certification, and outcome guarantees are each described in ordinary operational terms.

A reader can conduct a minimum Focus episode from `README.md:35-60` or `framework/practice-guide.md:8-62` without reading every document or adopting a specialized tool.

## Cognitive-burden and non-coercion results

The six concerns are presented as subjects that remain clear, and the seven moves as actions that may be used, skipped, repeated, or stopped. The package explicitly says they are not thirteen steps, a lifecycle, a score, or a completion gate.

Checklists, matrices, tables, Review Items, Transition Check-Ins, Reflection, Journal, AI, paper, digital tools, Personal Mission Control, and connectors remain optional or purpose-bounded. Paper-only, digital-only, unassisted, and AI-assisted paths are all valid. No dashboard, fixed cadence, streak, score, hidden rank, urgency rule, zero-inbox target, or completion-at-all-costs language controls attention.

Deferral, release, dismissal, leaving unchanged, deletion where appropriate, stop, and no action recur as legitimate outcomes. Review Items expose source, freshness, why now, choices, authority, and boundaries; reviews can be bounded by time, item count, context, or decision type and may end through dismissal.

The owner-pilot record is long but does not make the pilot a release prerequisite or a target to finish at any cost. It prohibits fabricated or private entries, supports sanitized observations, provides a linear alternative to its widest table, permits paths to overlap, does not require an external mutation, and directs the owner to stop or reshape on burden, coercion, privacy, sharing, overwrite, authority, or unauthorized-action failures. Pilot coverage requirements do not make those practices mandatory for ordinary Focus use.

Safety and misuse warnings are direct and paired with bounded responses such as narrowing, stopping, correcting, disconnecting, or continuing manually. They do not require constant vigilance.

## Inclusive-scope and accessibility results

Focus serves one individual without assuming a team role, management position, job type, family structure, or complete life model. Work, personal, and combined scopes are selectively chosen and may remain separate. Examples span household, work, creative, reflective, physical, digital, interruption, and recovery contexts without making any one context normative.

Paper is first-class but not mandatory. Digital tools, online services, AI, connectors, image extraction, and voice are all optional. Spoken capture is described as deliberate foreground capture and never as ambient recording or an assumed capability.

Time bounds in practical reviews and examples are chosen for the situation, may be replaced by item, context, or decision-type bounds, and do not become universal timing rules. The three-week pilot duration is a specific evidence protocol, not a minimum Focus practice.

The package excludes medical, therapeutic, crisis, diagnostic, legal, employment, and wellbeing-treatment guidance and makes no treatment, safety, wellbeing, or performance guarantee.

The exact Commons adoption boundary is understandable and consistent with the adopted release. Commons owns shared principles and boundaries but not a product's purpose, audience, method, examples, Mission Control guidance, governance, release, or implementation. Focus adopts the exact release while retaining those local authorities, does not claim current Commons ecosystem membership, and requires a separate Commons change for later recognition. No Commons diagram or product list is imported as parent doctrine.

## Findings

### APL-001

1. **ID:** APL-001
2. **Severity:** Minor
3. **Location:** `AGENTS.md:23-26`; `README.md:82-85`; `framework/charter.md:176-179`; `GOVERNANCE.md:78-80`; `project/specifications/v1.0.0.md:21-23`; `SECURITY.md:11-13`
4. **Evidence:** Five Commons commit links use only the 40-character commit identifier as link text. The private vulnerability-reporting link uses the full URL as its visible label. Their surrounding sentences identify the destinations, but the link text alone does not.
5. **Requirement or risk:** Link labels should explain their destinations without depending on surrounding prose. Raw identifiers and URLs create unnecessary navigation burden, especially when links are encountered through a links list or assistive reading surface.
6. **Impact:** A reader navigating by links must listen to or parse a long identifier or URL and then recover the purpose from surrounding text. The destinations remain discoverable and safe, so this does not make a required path unusable.
7. **Smallest acceptable correction:** Give each Commons commit link a label such as “Commons commit `a0f0d384e9010a65d1a21a324b4c912433d5e031`,” and label the security URL “private vulnerability-reporting form,” while preserving the exact destinations and identifiers.
8. **Rerun required:** Yes. Correcting these candidate Markdown files creates a new content candidate and requires the accessibility and plain-language review to rerun.

## Limitations
