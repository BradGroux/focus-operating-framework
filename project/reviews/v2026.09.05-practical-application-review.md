## Cross-cutting tests

- **Low energy and no cue:** `framework/practice-guide.md`, “Return without
  catching up,” permits available context, one smaller step, ordinary accessible
  surfaces or no new record. Missing controlling evidence pauses the affected
  consequential decision while rest or unrelated safe work can continue.
- **Fragmented days and missed routines:** No uninterrupted block, reconstruction
  of missing days, backfilled Check-Ins or cleared Review Items is required.
- **Rest and abandonment:** Rest needs no productivity justification; an abandoned
  private plan needs neither a return path nor a reason. Records still needed
  for another purpose remain protected.
- **External constraints:** Releasing an intention cannot cancel a shared
  commitment. A requested date or scope change remains separate from the
  controlling commitment until its authority confirms it. No successful
  renegotiation is assumed.
- **Journal distinctions:** Check-Ins are not automatically Journal Entries;
  Reflection can remain on its original surface or in memory. Journal admission
  and resurfacing remain explicit and bounded.
- **Focus Review:** Source, freshness, why now, authority, boundaries and choices
  are inspectable; time or item bounds and dismissal prevent a closure target.
- **Assistance and connectors:** The individual chooses admitted material and
  accepts bounded proposals. Separate observation, sensitive reading, drafting,
  changing, sending and deleting permissions never approve a specific action.
  AI cannot execute external mutations after confirmation.
- **Receipts:** Existing destination evidence is sufficient only when the exact
  request, result and readback remain inspectable. Missing results remain
  unknown and do not authorize retries.
- **Exit:** Usable accepted context, source relationships and corrections must
  survive disconnection. An opaque export is insufficient; a derived view cannot
  be the only usable copy.
