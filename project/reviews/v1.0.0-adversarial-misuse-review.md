## Limitations

- This is a documentation misuse review of one exact content candidate, not a review of any implementation, connector, storage design, access-control system, or operational deployment.
- The review can determine whether the public package supplies a plausible textual justification for misuse. It cannot ensure that implementers or practitioners follow the package.
- Recovery guidance was assessed for clarity and coverage, not exercised against a running system.
- The adopted Commons release was used only for its shared principles, boundaries, vocabulary, governance, and evidence rules. Its review records were excluded.
- A GO is not a security certification, medical or wellbeing claim, conformance mark, or evidence of real-world or general effectiveness.
