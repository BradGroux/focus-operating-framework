## Findings

No Blocker, Material, Minor, or Suggestion finding was identified. The strongest
hostile readings depend on isolated enabling phrases while ignoring controlling
requirements that are explicit, repeated across authority levels, and backed by
clear failure or stop conditions. A practical adopter could still build a system
that performs any of the twenty misuses, but could not reasonably claim that the
misuse conforms to this exact Focus package.

## Limitations

This was a documentation-only adversarial review of one exact candidate. It did
not test an implementation, connector, provider, user interface, operational
deployment, real-world adoption, or practitioner outcome. It does not establish
security, privacy, accessibility, legal, medical, clinical, professional,
technical-conformance, certification, or effectiveness claims. The GO verdict
applies only to whether the public text materially defeats the twenty required
hostile interpretations for the exact target.

## Verdict

**GO.** No unresolved adversarial interpretation materially defeats the
framework's one-individual scope, selective admission, source and authority
separation, Journal privacy, optional and advisory AI boundary, separately
controlled external action, optional Mission Control, portable exit, evidence
limits, or documentation-only scope. This verdict is not a security
certification or effectiveness claim.
