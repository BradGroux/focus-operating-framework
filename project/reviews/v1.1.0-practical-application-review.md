## Explicit checks passed with no finding

- The exact candidate commit and tree, clean state, absent generated v1.1.0
  review records, absent destination, and exact Commons tag, commit, and tree
  passed preflight.
- The charter retains ten commitments; canonical guidance retains six concerns
  and seven optional moves without a mandatory sequence or completion gate.
- All seven examples state fictional and non-authoritative status, purpose, life
  scope, assumptions, limits, concerns and moves, provenance and authority,
  boundaries, an illustrative outcome, and lessons without general
  effectiveness claims.
- All 56 candidate Markdown files passed local-link and heading-target
  validation.
- Paper-only and unassisted paths remain complete and respected rather than
  reduced fallbacks.
- Selective admission, foreground capture, retained source identity, visible
  correction, and purpose-specific authority remain usable across physical and
  digital surfaces.
- AI receives only admitted material for the current purpose, cites support,
  exposes uncertainty, and leaves consequential decisions to the individual.
- External-action previews, exact human control, distinct connector authority,
  and Action Receipts prevent permission or assistance from becoming approval.
- Shared promotion remains bounded and excludes originating private context.
- Stale projections, disagreement, tool failure, provider exit, and deletion
  uncertainty remain visible rather than being silently reconciled.
- The adopted Commons release supports people-first, tool-replaceable,
  independent, honest-learning boundaries without taking ownership of Focus's
  local method.

## Limitations

- This was a documentation application review, not observed real-world use.
- No implementation, connector, account, provider behavior, or external action
  was executed.
- The review assessed whether the documented paths are understandable and
  internally applicable; it did not measure focus, output, wellbeing, safety,
  compliance, or effectiveness.
- Commons material was used only to test the exact adoption boundary and
  compatibility, not as product-specific Focus guidance.

## Verdict

**GO.** The exact target is practically coherent for all seven required
application paths and the cross-cutting tests. It gives one individual enough
clear guidance to apply Focus while preserving choice, authority, privacy,
provenance, and manual continuity, subject to the limitations above. This
verdict applies only to the exact commit and tree reviewed and does not claim
real-world effectiveness.
