## Cross-cutting test results

- **Life scope and individual authority: PASS.** Work, personal, and combined
  scopes are explicit and selective. The charter, practice guide, Mission
  Control guidance, and examples consistently exclude teams, assignments,
  manager views, shared Focus workspaces, and whole-life capture.
- **Transition Check-In, Reflection, and Journal Entry: PASS.** A Check-In is
  optional and normally ephemeral; a Reflection may remain on its original
  surface; and a Check-In becomes a Journal Entry only through explicit
  promotion into a Journal designated for that purpose.
- **Focus Review: PASS.** Review Items expose source, freshness, why now,
  choices, authority, and boundaries. Reviews are individually chosen and
  bounded, and dismissal, non-action, and stopping are valid outcomes.
- **Personal Mission Control: PASS.** It is optional, bounded, derived,
  replaceable, non-authoritative, and available through paper, ordinary files,
  several tools, or no composed view at all.
- **Private-to-shared boundary: PASS.** Shared promotion requires a separate,
  previewed, minimal artifact. Originating Check-Ins, Reflections, Journal
  material, and decision history stay excluded unless explicitly selected with
  appropriate authority.
- **Authority and conflict: PASS.** Records of authority are designated by
  purpose. Sources, derivatives, proposals, accepted records, and projections
  retain distinct status. Corrections preserve provenance, and conflicts remain
  visible instead of using silent merge, overwrite, or latest-timestamp-wins.
- **Connector and external-action boundary: PASS.** Basic observation,
  sensitive reading, drafting, changing, sending, and deleting are separate
  authority levels. Permission does not approve a use. Every external mutation
  requires an exact preview, controlling source and consequential-detail check,
  human confirmation, separate human or connector execution, and an Action
  Receipt with destination readback or visible failure. AI cannot execute or
  retry the mutation.
- **Portable exit and manual continuity: PASS.** Accepted records, relations,
  corrections, provenance, and references must remain usable outside a
  supporting tool. Stale or unavailable projections fail visibly, connectors
  can be disconnected without destroying unrelated authority, and practice can
  continue through paper or ordinary files.
- **Completion and stopping: PASS.** A Focus episode completes when the person
  can continue or choose a responsible non-action outcome with enough truth and
  authority. Burden, exposure, coercion, unclear authority, provider dependence,
  or a simpler adequate cue are explicit reasons to narrow, reshape, or stop.
- **Commons adoption boundary: PASS.** Focus pins the exact Commons v1.1.0
  release, preserves product independence, and keeps Focus-specific method,
  terminology, examples, governance, evidence, licensing, releases, and
  implementation choices under Focus authority. No adoption conflict was found.

## Explicit checks that passed with no finding

- The framework can be applied without inventing software, a hidden workflow,
  a universal authority, or a mandatory sequence.
- All seven examples state fictional, illustrative, and non-authoritative
  status; purpose; life scope; assumptions; limits; concerns and moves;
  provenance and authority decisions; boundaries; an illustrative outcome; and
  lessons without a general-effectiveness claim.
- Each required path supports selective admission rather than capture-everything
  and preserves legitimate defer, release, dismissal, stop, or no-action
  outcomes where applicable.
- Paper-only practice is complete without digitization, and every assisted path
  preserves a complete, respected unassisted path.
- Optional AI context is purpose-bounded; proposals identify source support,
  remain visibly unaccepted, and require human review of consequential details.
- No AI path grants authority to promote, change, send, publish, cancel, or
  delete an external record.
- Records of authority remain separate by purpose across mixed life scope and
  across physical and digital surfaces.
- Projections expose freshness and conflict, and neither convenience nor newest
  timestamp silently establishes authority.
- Provider failure and exit leave an understandable manual path and usable
  context rather than only an opaque export.

## Limitations

This was a documentation application review of an exact candidate, not an
observed practice trial or implementation test. The examples are fictional and
cannot establish effectiveness, safety, suitability, reduced restart effort,
or successful operation in every real external system. Actual privacy,
retention, export, deletion, accessibility, and destination-readback behavior
will depend on the materials and systems an individual chooses; the framework
appropriately requires those conditions to be inspected rather than assuming
them.

## Verdict

**GO.** The exact target is practically coherent for the seven required paths
and the cross-cutting tests, subject to the stated limitations. This verdict
does not claim real-world effectiveness.
