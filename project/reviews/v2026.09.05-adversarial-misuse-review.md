## Recovery tests

The documents provide an actionable stop or narrowing response before misuse is
normalized: preserve visible conflict; return to the controlling source; narrow
admission; remove a projection or derivative without destroying authority;
disconnect a connector; recover retained accepted context and provenance in
usable forms; continue unassisted; and defer, rest, release or stop.

Two adverse cases received additional checks. First, releasing a private plan
does not erase an external obligation: practice-guide recovery and example 07
keep a requested renegotiation unresolved until authority confirms it. Second,
a failed or unknown external mutation does not permit a retry: AI receipt rules
require checking the destination and separately authorizing any retry. No
unseen deletion or guaranteed recovery is claimed.

Governance supplies an issue and accountable decision path for contradictions,
with only the disputed action paused and uncertainty and dissent retained.
Commons human-boundary clarifications do not create permission to disregard
external authority, disclose private material or impose participation.
