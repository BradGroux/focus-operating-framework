# Focus Operating Framework charter

- **Status:** Accepted v1.1.0
- **Founding steward:** Brad Groux
- **Effective date:** 2026-08-22

## Definition

The Focus Operating Framework is an open individual operating framework for
directing attention and preserving continuity across the work and personal
contexts a person chooses. It combines physical and digital practice, supports
complete unassisted and AI-assisted paths, and keeps the individual
authoritative.

Focus is a documentation framework. It does not require an application,
account, provider, device, model, connector, database, or prescribed physical
material.

## Mission

Help individuals carry intention and context through transitions, choose what
deserves attention, continue meaningful work, and learn without surrendering
privacy, records, or judgment to a tool.

## Who it serves

Focus serves one individual. That individual chooses whether the practice
covers work life, personal life, or both. Work-related material remains part of
the individual's private practice unless a bounded artifact is deliberately
promoted into a shared external record.

Focus does not define team ownership, organizational workflow, assignments,
manager views, shared Focus workspaces, or a business operating system. A
combined life scope does not authorize whole-life capture.

## First job

After an interruption or context switch, Focus helps the individual recover:

- why the chosen work matters;
- where it stands; and
- the next chosen step.

The intended result is less restart effort. Focus calls this **Focus
continuity**. It does not require uninterrupted attention, constant activity,
or completion.

## Commitments

1. **The individual remains authoritative.** Tools and assistance never own
   attention, records, or actions.
2. **Attention is chosen, not assigned.** Rankings and reminders may inform but
   cannot decide what matters.
3. **Continuity matters more than activity volume.** Preserve enough context to
   return; do not reward constant capture or completion.
4. **Paper is first-class.** Physical practice remains valid without
   digitization, destruction, or proprietary materials.
5. **Admission is selective.** Bring in only what serves a stated purpose.
6. **Sources remain distinct from derivatives.** Correction and promotion never
   erase provenance.
7. **Every move is optional and contextual.** Deferring, releasing, stopping,
   and taking no action are legitimate.
8. **AI is optional, integral when chosen, and advisory.** Consequential
   interpretation and action remain human decisions.
9. **Tools remain replaceable.** Accepted records support portable exit and
   manual continuity.
10. **Learning must not become judgment.** Reflection improves the practice
    without streaks, shame, productivity scores, or universal outcome claims.

## Method

Focus defines six concerns that an individual keeps clear:

- Intention;
- Attention;
- Continuity;
- Authority;
- Boundaries; and
- Learning.

Focus also defines seven flexible moves:

- Orient;
- Capture;
- Clarify;
- Connect;
- Choose;
- Continue; and
- Reflect.

The concerns are not departments, stages, or scores. The moves are not a
lifecycle. A person may enter anywhere, skip or repeat a move, use only what the
situation needs, remain on one surface, or stop.

The canonical definitions and application guidance are in
[concerns and moves](concerns-and-moves.md).

## Scope

The framework covers:

- individual intention and chosen attention;
- continuity through interruption, transition, tool failure, and provider exit;
- work, personal, or combined life scope chosen by the individual;
- foreground, selective, purpose-specific capture;
- paper-only, digital-only, and physical-digital practice;
- explicit sources, derivatives, proposals, accepted records, projections,
  provenance, freshness, conflict, and records of authority;
- deliberate promotion into a named record of authority;
- optional Transition Check-Ins, Reflection, Journal, Focus Review, and Personal
  Mission Control;
- complete unassisted and AI-assisted practice;
- optional, separately controlled connectors and Action Receipts;
- bounded shared promotion from a private practice; and
- learning from outcomes and friction without scoring the individual.

## Anti-scope

The framework does not:

- define a team, organizational, or business operating framework;
- replace a task manager, project manager, calendar, relationship-management
  system, knowledge system, Journal, or external record of authority;
- prescribe universal productivity, performance optimization, or a complete
  model of a person's life;
- provide medical, therapeutic, crisis, diagnostic, legal, employment, or
  wellness-treatment guidance;
- require paper, digitization, AI, image extraction, voice, connectors, a
  dashboard, or any particular tool;
- authorize ambient capture, passive surveillance, watched libraries, bulk
  ingestion, or whole-life indexing;
- allow AI or an autonomous agent to accept consequential details, decide
  attention, or execute an external mutation;
- create shared Focus records, team assignments, manager dashboards, employee
  monitoring, personal scores, streaks, or shame states;
- define software architecture, application code, schemas, protocols,
  provider-specific connectors, automation, CI, runtime behavior, or technical
  conformance;
- establish a maturity model, certification, or universal lifecycle; or
- guarantee focus, output, wellbeing, safety, compliance, or success.

## Authority, provenance, and private practice

The individual designates the record of authority for each purpose. Different
purposes may have different authoritative records. Focus itself is not a
universal source of truth, and a derived view is not authoritative because it
is convenient or current-looking.

A source retains its identity when an image, transcription, cleaned passage,
summary, proposal, projection, or promoted record is created from it. A
derivative does not become authoritative by existing. Promotion requires human
approval, a named destination, preserved provenance, and visible success or
failure. Conflicts remain visible until accountable review resolves them;
silent merge, silent overwrite, and latest-timestamp-wins are prohibited.

The individual's sources, Check-Ins, Reflections, Journal, choices, and
supporting history form a private practice. Shared subject matter does not make
that private context shared. Only a deliberately selected, previewed artifact
may be promoted to an external shared record.

## Assistance and external action

Unassisted practice is complete and canonical. AI-assisted practice is also
complete and canonical when the individual chooses it. AI may work only with
material deliberately admitted for the current purpose. Its output remains a
source-backed proposal or draft until the individual reviews it.

AI cannot execute an external mutation, including after the individual confirms
a proposal. The individual either acts directly or separately invokes a
controlled connector. Observation, sensitive reading, drafting, changing,
sending, and deleting are distinct connector authority levels. Permission does
not approve a particular action.

## Commons adoption and independence

Focus adopts [Open Framework Commons
v1.1.0](https://github.com/BradGroux/open-framework-commons/tree/v1.1.0)
exactly. The adopted annotated tag peels to
[`f25a2b89b4aed95984fd235e2e229efe52c125d8`](https://github.com/BradGroux/open-framework-commons/commit/f25a2b89b4aed95984fd235e2e229efe52c125d8).

Commons supplies shared ecosystem principles and boundaries. Focus owns its
local purpose, audience, commitments, concerns, moves, practices, terminology,
examples, governance, and releases. A change in either repository has no
automatic authority over the other.

Commons v1.1.0 recognizes Focus as an independent ecosystem product with equal
provenance and importance. That recognition does not transfer authority:
Focus remains independently governed and owns its purpose, audience, method,
terminology, evidence, releases, licensing, and implementation choices.

## Implementations

An implementation may support Focus, including a future Personal Mission
Control application, but it remains a non-authoritative example implementation.
It must identify the exact Focus release it applies. Its screens, data model,
automation, storage, provider, connector, and commercial choices cannot redefine
the framework.

## Accountability and amendment

Brad Groux is the founding steward and release authority. Contributors and
reviewers may apply, challenge, draft, and evaluate the framework but gain no
implicit authority to change its meaning.

The charter is the highest public authority in this repository. An amendment
requires a public proposal, consequences and compatibility analysis, review
against the adopted Commons release and Focus boundaries, documented material
dissent, explicit steward approval, and an identifiable semantic release. No
example, pilot result, implementation, tool behavior, repeated practice, or
review report may amend the charter silently.
