# Concerns and moves

Focus separates what must remain clear from what a person may do. The six
**concerns** are persistent subjects of judgment. The seven **moves** are
optional actions. Neither list creates a sequence, lifecycle, score, maturity
model, screen set, or completion gate.

Use the smallest combination that helps the present situation. A person may
start with Choose, return to Clarify, remain paper-only, or stop after Orient.

## Six concerns

### Intention

**Definition:** Why something matters, what the individual is choosing, and
when stopping is a valid choice.

Ask:

- What am I trying to preserve, decide, continue, or release?
- Whose intention is this?
- What would make stopping, narrowing, or reconsidering responsible?

Keep visible:

- the individual's current purpose;
- material constraints and commitments; and
- the difference between a chosen intention and an inherited demand.

Do not reduce Intention to a goal score, imposed purpose, or endless
optimization target.

### Attention

**Definition:** What deserves focus now, why now, and what the individual
deliberately defers or releases.

Ask:

- What deserves attention now, and what is the source of that claim?
- Why is it being presented now?
- What can be deferred, released, left unchanged, or ignored?

Keep visible:

- source and freshness;
- why-now rationale;
- available choices, including no action; and
- the individual's final decision.

Do not treat an algorithmic rank, reminder volume, activity count, or repeated
prompt as authority.

### Continuity

**Definition:** The context needed to pause and resume across interruptions,
transitions, physical and digital surfaces, and tool failure.

Ask:

- What will I need to understand when I return?
- Where does the authoritative context live?
- Can I continue if this surface or provider disappears?

Keep visible:

- why the work matters;
- where it stands;
- the next chosen step;
- unresolved uncertainty; and
- a manual recovery path.

Do not equate continuity with constant capture, uninterrupted work, or complete
synchronization.

### Authority

**Definition:** Clarity about which source, record, and person controls and how
proposals become accepted or actions become authorized.

Ask:

- What is the source?
- Which record controls for this specific purpose?
- Is this item a derivative, proposal, accepted record, or projection?
- Who may accept, promote, or act?

Keep visible:

- record of authority by purpose;
- provenance and corrections;
- unresolved conflicts;
- connector authority level; and
- exact human approval and Action Receipt for external mutation.

Do not treat permission as approval, a derivative as truth, or the newest copy
as automatically authoritative.

### Boundaries

**Definition:** The limits on what may be admitted, retained, exposed,
automated, or removed, including the individual's cognitive and privacy limits.

Ask:

- What minimum material serves this purpose?
- What should remain on its original surface?
- Who or what may see it, for how long, and why?
- What burden, privacy cost, or coercion means the practice should stop?

Keep visible:

- purpose of admission;
- retention and deletion choices;
- provider exposure;
- private versus shared context;
- sensitive or third-party authority; and
- disconnect and portable-exit behavior.

Do not pursue capture completeness, unlimited context, invisible automation, or
whole-life indexing.

### Learning

**Definition:** The use of reflection, correction, outcomes, and friction to
improve the individual's Focus practice without scoring or shame.

Ask:

- Did this reduce restart effort, confusion, or risk?
- What review or correction burden did it create?
- What should be corrected, simplified, retained, or stopped?
- Is the observation local experience or stronger evidence?

Keep visible:

- actual outcome and burden;
- limitations and uncertainty;
- privacy or authority failures; and
- a proportionate practice adjustment.

Do not convert Reflection into productivity ranking, streak pressure, a mood
score, or a universal outcome claim.

## Seven moves

### Orient

Name the present context, intention, constraints, and valid stopping point.

Useful when entering, returning, or noticing that a situation has changed.
Orient can end the episode if there is no responsible next action.

Minimum result:

- a truthful present context;
- why it matters now; and
- a limit or stopping condition.

### Capture

Selectively preserve a physical, digital, or spoken source with enough
provenance for its stated purpose.

Capture is foreground and optional. A paper mark can be the complete result. A
deliberate spoken note can be captured without authorizing ambient recording.

Minimum result:

- a stated purpose;
- a source identity; and
- proportionate retention and exposure.

### Clarify

Review derivatives, correct uncertainty, and separate source facts from
proposals.

Clarify does not erase the source or make cleaned text authoritative. It records
what was checked, corrected, left uncertain, and accepted or rejected.

Minimum result:

- visible distinctions among source, derivative, and proposal; and
- consequential details left for human confirmation.

### Connect

Relate selected material to useful context or explicitly promote it into a
record of authority.

A relation can remain private and non-authoritative. Promotion is a distinct
human-approved act with a named destination and preserved provenance.

Minimum result:

- a useful relation or an explicit decision not to connect; and
- if promoted, a destination and visible result.

### Choose

Decide what deserves attention now and what to defer, release, leave unchanged,
or stop.

Choose uses source, freshness, why now, intention, constraints, and available
capacity. The decision belongs to the individual.

Minimum result:

- a chosen next step or a legitimate non-action outcome; and
- no hidden claim that the choice was objectively optimal.

### Continue

Resume or perform chosen work while leaving enough context for the next
transition.

Continue is not a requirement to finish. It may produce a small action, a
bounded shared promotion, a new physical source, or a deliberate pause.

Minimum result:

- progress or responsible restraint; and
- enough continuity for the next return when needed.

### Reflect

Examine outcomes and friction, correct records, and adapt the practice
proportionately.

Reflection may remain on its original surface. It becomes a Journal Entry only
through deliberate admission into a Journal designated as authoritative for
Journal Entries. When the Reflection began as a Transition Check-In, that
admission requires explicit promotion.

Minimum result:

- a truthful observation; and
- a local adjustment, correction, open question, or decision to leave the
  practice unchanged.

## Concerns and moves work together

Any move may engage several concerns. This matrix gives common relationships,
not required coverage.

| Move | Concerns often made clearer | Typical evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orient | Intention, Attention, Boundaries | Present context, why now, constraint, stopping point |
| Capture | Continuity, Authority, Boundaries | Source identity, purpose, retention, exposure |
| Clarify | Authority, Continuity, Learning | Corrections, uncertainty, proposal status, source spans |
| Connect | Continuity, Authority, Boundaries | Relation, promotion decision, destination, provenance |
| Choose | Intention, Attention, Boundaries | Chosen step, defer/release/no-action decision, why now |
| Continue | Attention, Continuity, Authority | Work performed, bounded promotion, next continuity cue |
| Reflect | Learning, Boundaries, Intention | Outcome, burden, failure, correction, practice change |

## Valid combinations

- **Paper-only reset:** Orient, Choose, and Continue on one sheet. No Capture
  into a digital system is required.
- **Return after interruption:** Orient from an existing continuity cue, Clarify
  what changed, then Choose. A new record may be unnecessary.
- **Physical-digital round trip:** Capture selected paper, Clarify a literal
  derivative, Connect an accepted passage, Continue with a new physical prompt,
  and later Reflect.
- **Focus Review:** Orient to current capacity, Clarify Review Items, Choose
  among action and non-action outcomes, and Reflect on burden.
- **Private Reflection:** Reflect on paper or digitally and stop. Journal
  promotion is optional.

Examples can show a complete path, but repeated use does not turn that path into
the framework's required order.

## Completion and stopping

A Focus episode is complete when the individual has enough clarity to continue,
defer, release, stop, or take no action responsibly. Completion does not require
all six concerns, all seven moves, a durable record, a digital artifact, a
Journal Entry, an AI proposal, or an external action.

Stop or reshape the practice when its review and correction burden equals or
exceeds its value, authority remains unclear, private context becomes exposed,
the individual feels coerced by reminders or scores, or a simpler existing note
provides the same continuity.
