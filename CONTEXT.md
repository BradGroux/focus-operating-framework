# Focus Operating Framework Context

The Focus Operating Framework is an open individual operating framework for
directing attention and preserving continuity across the work and personal
contexts a person chooses. Its language describes the tool-agnostic framework
and keeps implementations subordinate to it.

## Language

**Focus Operating Framework**:
An independent, tool-agnostic individual operating framework that combines
physical and digital practice, supports unassisted and AI-assisted paths, and
keeps the individual authoritative. Its meaning is owned by its canonical
documents and governance.
_Avoid_: Focus app, productivity software, operating system

**Example implementation**:
A non-authoritative, tool-specific application that demonstrates one way to
apply an exact Focus Operating Framework release.
_Avoid_: Canonical app, required app, reference standard

**Practice example**:
A bounded, non-authoritative illustration of selected Focus concerns and moves
in one individual context.
_Avoid_: Required workflow, effectiveness proof, implementation specification

**Complex case study**:
A non-authoritative, end-to-end illustration that combines multiple Focus
concerns, moves, life contexts, surfaces, and authority boundaries.
_Avoid_: Canonical journey, complete life model, validated outcome

**Focus episode**:
A bounded instance in which an individual applies one or more Focus moves to a
real situation and may continue, defer, release, or stop.
_Avoid_: Software event, completed workflow, productivity unit

**Owner pilot**:
Pre-release use of the framework by its steward across representative Focus
episodes to find friction, contradictions, and unsafe boundaries. It is
practice evidence, not general validation.
_Avoid_: User study, market validation, effectiveness proof

**Commons adoption**:
The framework's explicit decision to reference an exact Open Framework Commons
release while retaining its own purpose, method, terminology, governance, and
release authority.
_Avoid_: Commons membership, inheritance, parent framework

**Focus continuity**:
The ability to recover why chosen work matters, where it stands, and the next
chosen step after an interruption or context switch with less restart effort.
_Avoid_: Uninterrupted attention, productivity score, activity volume

**Physical-digital round trip**:
An optional practice that moves selected physical thinking into a reviewed
digital derivative and later returns a prompt or reflection to a new linked
physical source. It is the flagship v1 example, not a required workflow.
_Avoid_: Mandatory digitization, paper replacement, notebook ingestion

**Physical prompt**:
A minimal cue returned to paper that points to useful accepted context, an
unresolved question, or a next chosen step without copying an entire digital
record.
_Avoid_: Paper synchronization, required printout, complete duplicate

**Linked physical response**:
A new physical source created in response to an earlier physical prompt or
related accepted context. It retains its own identity and provenance.
_Avoid_: Edit to the original source, synchronized paper copy

**Record of authority**:
The artifact or system explicitly designated as controlling for a particular
purpose. Different purposes may have different records of authority.
_Avoid_: Universal source of truth, newest copy

**Source**:
The physical or digital artifact from which a derivative, proposal, or
promotion originates. A source retains its identity when later representations
are created.
_Avoid_: Input blob, disposable original

**Derivative**:
A representation produced from a source, such as an image, transcription, or
cleaned passage. A derivative does not become authoritative merely by existing.
_Avoid_: Replacement, canonical copy

**Literal derivative**:
A close representation of a source that preserves uncertainty, visible
structure, and non-text elements rather than silently interpreting or cleaning
them.
_Avoid_: Polished summary, corrected source, inferred transcription

**Corrected derivative**:
A human-reviewed derivative whose corrections and remaining uncertainty are
recorded without rewriting the source or earlier derivative.
_Avoid_: Replacement source, silent cleanup, automatically accepted text

**Accepted record**:
Selected material deliberately accepted into a named record of authority for a
stated purpose. Acceptance does not make the record authoritative for another
purpose or erase its provenance.
_Avoid_: Proposal, newest copy, universal truth

**Provenance**:
The traceable relationship among a source, its derivatives, human review, and
any accepted promotion.
_Avoid_: Unexplained metadata, inferred history

**Promotion**:
A human-approved placement of selected material into a named record of
authority while preserving its provenance.
_Avoid_: Automatic filing, silent synchronization

**Projection**:
A replaceable view or cached representation of material whose record of
authority remains elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Authoritative copy, owned record

**Freshness**:
The visible degree to which a projection is known to reflect its record of
authority at a stated time.
_Avoid_: Presumed current, live by default

**Conflict**:
A visible disagreement among sources, derivatives, accepted records, or
projections that requires accountable review rather than automatic resolution.
_Avoid_: Latest wins, silent merge

**Portable exit**:
The ability to recover accepted records, relations, corrections, and external
references in usable forms, disconnect supporting tools, and continue the
practice without a single provider.
_Avoid_: Data dump, vendor migration promise

**Selective admission**:
The intentional inclusion of only the material needed for a stated Focus
purpose. Admission is optional, proportionate, and does not imply permanent
retention or broader use.
_Avoid_: Capture everything, whole-life indexing, automatic ingestion

**Admitted material**:
A source or derivative that a practitioner has deliberately brought into a
Focus practice for a stated purpose.
_Avoid_: All available context, ambient data, provider-accessible data

**Transition Check-In**:
A minimal, optional note made at a context shift that records what is happening
now and the next chosen step. It may be ephemeral and may end without action.
_Avoid_: Status report, mandatory log, journal entry

**Reflection**:
Intentional consideration used for later understanding. It may remain on its
original surface or be admitted into a Journal under a separately chosen
retention and resurfacing policy.
_Avoid_: Automatic journal, mood score, activity streak

**Journal**:
An optional, private, durable collection of the individual's intentional
Reflections. It becomes a record of authority for Journal Entries only when the
individual explicitly designates it for that purpose.
_Avoid_: Activity log, mandatory daily record, universal memory

**Journal Entry**:
A durable Reflection deliberately admitted into a Journal. A Transition
Check-In becomes a Journal Entry only through explicit promotion into a Journal
that the individual has designated as the relevant record of authority.
_Avoid_: Check-In, automatic transcript, permanent capture

**Individual**:
The one person who owns a Focus practice and decides its scope, records of
authority, admissions, promotions, retention, and supporting tools.
_Avoid_: User account, workspace, team, organization

**Focus practice**:
An individual's application of the framework to maintain focus continuity
across the selected parts of work life, personal life, or both.
_Avoid_: Team workflow, business operating system, whole-life database

**Life scope**:
The work, personal, or combined parts of life that an individual chooses to
support with a Focus practice. Life scope permits selective admission; it does
not make every available detail part of Focus.
_Avoid_: Workspace scope, capture everything, complete life record

**Capture**:
The deliberate creation or admission of a physical, digital, or spoken source
for a stated Focus purpose. Capture is foreground, optional, and
surface-neutral.
_Avoid_: Ambient recording, background collection, bulk ingestion

**Private practice**:
The individual's Focus context, including Check-Ins, Reflections, sources,
derivatives, history, and supporting decisions. Work-related subject matter
does not make the practice shared.
_Avoid_: Personal workspace, team knowledge base, manager view

**Shared promotion**:
A deliberate, previewed promotion of a bounded artifact into an external shared
record of authority. It does not expose the originating private practice unless
the individual explicitly includes selected context.
_Avoid_: Workspace sync, shared Focus record, automatic publication

**Personal Mission Control**:
An optional, stack-agnostic Focus pattern that composes selected intentions,
attention, continuity cues, Check-Ins, Reflections, Review Items, projections,
promotions, and Action Receipts for one individual. It is a derived view, not a
record of authority.
_Avoid_: Required dashboard, everything app, team command center

**Unassisted practice**:
A complete Focus practice performed by the individual without AI assistance.
_Avoid_: Manual fallback, reduced mode

**AI-assisted practice**:
A first-class, optional Focus practice in which the individual chooses AI
assistance for admitted material while retaining decision and action authority.
AI remains proposal- and draft-only and cannot execute an external mutation.
_Avoid_: Autonomous Focus, AI-required practice, add-on method

**Proposal**:
An unaccepted interpretation or suggestion produced through assistance. It has
no authority until the individual reviews and accepts it.
_Avoid_: Decision, accepted record, automatic update

**Consequential detail**:
A person, date, amount, commitment, destination, or action whose acceptance may
change an authoritative record or affect someone outside the private practice.
_Avoid_: Harmless metadata, inferred fact

**Connector**:
An optional, replaceable implementation adapter through which an individual
selectively observes or changes an external system under explicit authority.
_Avoid_: Framework dependency, synchronization authority, required integration

**Authority level**:
A separately granted class of connector capability, such as basic observation,
sensitive reading, drafting, changing, sending, or deleting.
_Avoid_: Connected, full access, permission equals approval

**External mutation**:
An explicitly approved creation, update, send, publication, cancellation, or
deletion in an external record of authority.
_Avoid_: Sync, suggestion, inferred intent

**Action Receipt**:
Evidence of the exact target, requested external mutation, result, and
destination readback or visible failure.
_Avoid_: Success message, activity log entry, assumed completion

**Review record**:
A publication-safe account of a review's exact target, scope, findings,
limitations, and disposition without naming private sources or review tools.
_Avoid_: Transcript, tool log, anonymous approval claim

**Review prompt**:
Reusable, tool-neutral instructions that identify an exact review target,
bounded evidence, review lens, finding format, limits, and stop conditions for
an assisting reviewer.
_Avoid_: Model prompt, private handoff, open-ended repository audit

**Publication-safe**:
Suitable for the public repository because it excludes private context,
credentials, source-inspiration history, and tool or model attribution while
preserving material decisions and evidence.
_Avoid_: Redacted-looking history, generated-by attribution, private provenance

**Focus Review**:
A bounded, individual-chosen practice for deciding what deserves attention now
and what requires review before acceptance, promotion, or action.
_Avoid_: Required Today screen, zero-inbox routine, fixed review schedule

**Review Item**:
A source-backed matter presented for an individual's decision, with visible
freshness, rationale, and available choices.
_Avoid_: Inbox task, notification, automatic priority

**Why now**:
The visible reason a Review Item is being presented at this time.
_Avoid_: Hidden ranking, unexplained urgency, engagement prompt

**Concern**:
A subject that an individual must keep clear while applying Focus, regardless
of the particular practice or sequence used.
_Avoid_: Stage, department, required screen

**Move**:
A flexible action an individual may use, skip, repeat, or stop while addressing
Focus concerns in context.
_Avoid_: Mandatory step, lifecycle phase, completion gate

## Concerns

**Intention**:
Why something matters, what the individual is choosing, and when stopping is a
valid choice.
_Avoid_: Goal score, imposed purpose, endless optimization

**Attention**:
What deserves focus now, why now, and what the individual deliberately defers
or releases.
_Avoid_: Automatic priority, activity volume, constant vigilance

**Continuity**:
The context needed to pause and resume across interruptions, transitions,
physical and digital surfaces, and tool failure.
_Avoid_: Constant capture, uninterrupted work, synchronized copy

**Authority**:
Clarity about which source, record, and person controls and how proposals become
accepted or actions become authorized.
_Avoid_: Permission implies approval, latest wins, automated ownership

**Boundaries**:
The limits on what may be admitted, retained, exposed, automated, or removed,
including the individual's cognitive and privacy limits.
_Avoid_: Capture completeness, unlimited context, invisible automation

**Learning**:
The use of reflection, correction, outcomes, and friction to improve the
individual's Focus practice without scoring or shame.
_Avoid_: Productivity ranking, streak pressure, universal outcome claim

## Moves

**Orient**:
Name the present context, intention, constraints, and valid stopping point.
_Avoid_: Mandatory setup, strategic planning phase

**Capture**:
Selectively preserve a physical, digital, or spoken source with enough
provenance for its purpose.
_Avoid_: Capture everything, automatic collection

**Clarify**:
Review derivatives, correct uncertainty, and separate source facts from
proposals.
_Avoid_: AI cleanup, automatic acceptance, silent correction

**Connect**:
Relate selected material to useful context or explicitly promote it into a
record of authority.
_Avoid_: Automatic filing, universal synchronization

**Choose**:
Decide what deserves attention now and what to defer, release, or leave
unchanged.
_Avoid_: Algorithmic priority, forced action

**Continue**:
Resume or perform chosen work while leaving enough context for the next
transition.
_Avoid_: Constant activity, finish-at-all-costs

**Reflect**:
Examine outcomes and friction, correct records, and adapt the practice
proportionately.
_Avoid_: Performance scoring, mandatory journaling
