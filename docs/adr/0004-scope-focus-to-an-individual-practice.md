# ADR 0004: Scope Focus to an individual practice

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-04

## Context

An individual moves across work and personal contexts that may involve shared
systems and other people. Expanding Focus itself into team ownership or business
operations would change whose attention and private context the framework
governs.

## Decision

Focus serves one individual's practice rather than a team, organization, or
business operating system. The individual may apply it to work life, personal
life, or both and retains authority over scope, admission, records, retention,
and tools.

Work-related material does not turn Focus into a team framework. Combined life
scope does not authorize whole-life capture. Focus may selectively observe an
external record or promote a bounded artifact into a shared system, but it does
not provide shared Focus workspaces, assignments, manager views, team
dashboards, or access to originating Check-Ins, Reflections, Journal material,
or broader private context.

## Consequences

- One individual remains accountable for attention and practice choices.
- Work and personal authority may remain in separate systems.
- Bounded shared promotion is supported without sharing the private practice.
- Multi-person product strategy is outside the initial framework.
