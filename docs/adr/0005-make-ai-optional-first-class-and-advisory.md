# ADR 0005: Make AI optional, first-class, and advisory

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-04

## Context

Treating AI as either mandatory or an undefined add-on would leave a major
practice path without clear authority, privacy, source, and action boundaries.
Assistance is useful only when the unassisted path remains complete and the
individual retains judgment.

## Decision

Focus v1 directly supports complete unassisted and AI-assisted practice. When
AI is chosen, it is an integral mode covered by canonical guidance.

AI may form source-backed proposals from explicitly admitted material. The
individual confirms consequential details and every promotion. AI remains
proposal- and draft-only and cannot execute an external mutation, including
after confirmation. The individual acts directly or separately invokes a
controlled connector. AI cannot silently change accepted records or treat
imported content as instructions.

## Consequences

- Unassisted practice is not a fallback or reduced mode.
- AI context is purpose-specific and selectively admitted.
- People, dates, amounts, commitments, destinations, and actions remain human
  decisions.
- External-action authority stays separate from assistance.
- The framework remains independent of any model, provider, or application.
