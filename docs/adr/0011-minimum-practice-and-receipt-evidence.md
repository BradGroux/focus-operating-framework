# ADR 0011: Minimum practice and receipt evidence

- **Status:** Accepted for edition 2026.09.05
- **Date:** 2026-09-05
- **Authority:** Brad Groux, founding steward and release authority
- **Proposal:** [Issue 8](https://github.com/BradGroux/focus-operating-framework/issues/8)

## Problem and decision

The prior recovery example assumes a retained cue, available authorities and a
ninety-minute work block. The practice guide allows stopping but does not explain
restarting with none of those resources or after a missed routine. The digital
resumption example requires a separate manual receipt despite destination
history. These are textual gaps and plausible burdens, not measured failures.

Make no-cue and no-catch-up recovery explicit, allow rest without justification,
and distinguish personal release from changing an external commitment. Ordinary
accessible surfaces and no new record remain valid. Existing destination
evidence may constitute an Action Receipt if it supplies the exact request,
result and readback; missing or uncertain evidence stays visible.

## Meaning and compatibility

This is substantive clarification and a relaxation of duplicate recordkeeping,
not a spelling-only correction. Readers and implementations requiring every cue
field, catch-up, continuous availability or a second receipt log should
reconsider those requirements. Exact preview, separate human external action,
source authority, privacy and unknown-result handling are unchanged. AI still
cannot execute external mutations, even after confirmation.

No concern, move or commitment is added or removed. The ten commitments remain
chosen values. Rest and ending a private plan support individual control, but
cannot cancel an external obligation or confer permission to delete records.
The strongest counterexample is a shared deadline that remains binding when
capacity falls: preserve its status and request a change without pretending it
has been accepted. Another is missing destination evidence: a receipt cannot
claim success merely to reduce burden.

## Alternatives, evidence and follow-up

Leaving the text unchanged retains ambiguous burdens. Removing all receipts
would lose accountable outcomes; compulsory new logs duplicate existing proof.
Use the smallest sufficient evidence instead. Fictional scenarios and independent
review test these interpretations; neither proves practical effectiveness or
specialist accessibility. The historical owner pilot remains NOT STARTED.

[Issue 10](https://github.com/BradGroux/focus-operating-framework/issues/10) stays
open for voluntary, actual practitioner evidence. The founding steward revisits
this guidance when permitted observations show coercion, lost commitments or
burden. No specialist suitability claim is authorized. Record any material
review dissent in the edition disposition rather than claiming unanimous field
agreement.
