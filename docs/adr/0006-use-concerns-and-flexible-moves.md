# ADR 0006: Use concerns and flexible moves

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-04

## Context

Focus needs enough structure for coherent practice without forcing every
situation through a universal lifecycle or completion state.

## Decision

Focus defines six concerns an individual keeps clear: Intention, Attention,
Continuity, Authority, Boundaries, and Learning.

Focus defines seven flexible moves that may be entered, skipped, repeated, or
stopped according to context: Orient, Capture, Clarify, Connect, Choose,
Continue, and Reflect.

The framework does not prescribe a universal lifecycle, maturity model, stage
sequence, score, certification, or required completion state. Examples may show
complete paths without turning those paths into requirements.

## Consequences

- An individual can start with the present need and use one or several moves.
- Paper-only and other bounded paths do not require unused moves.
- Defer, release, stop, and no action remain legitimate.
- Implementations cannot present screens or statuses as the canonical sequence.
