# ADR 0003: Require selective admission

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-04

## Context

Comprehensive capture can increase cognitive burden, privacy exposure,
correction work, and provider dependence while obscuring why material was
included. Availability alone is not a Focus purpose.

## Decision

Focus requires the individual to admit material intentionally for a stated
purpose and to keep only what that purpose needs. It rejects mandatory
digitization, passive or ambient capture, watched libraries, bulk ingestion,
whole-life indexing, and capture-completeness goals.

Use, retention, provider exposure, and deletion must be understandable.
Sensitive or third-party material requires separate justification and
appropriate authority or consent.

## Consequences

- Paper may remain entirely outside digital practice.
- Each assistance use receives only the context selected for its current
  purpose.
- Deletion and disconnection can occur without destroying unrelated authority.
- The framework accepts less speculative recall in exchange for clearer
  boundaries and lower burden.
