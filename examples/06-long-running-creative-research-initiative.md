# 06: Long-running creative research initiative

- **Status:** Fictional, illustrative, and non-authoritative complex case study
- **Purpose:** Preserve continuity across a six-month independent writing and
  research initiative
- **Life scope:** Personal creative work
- **Assumptions:** Paper supports ideation; ordinary digital files hold selected
  source notes and the authoritative manuscript
- **Limits:** No comprehensive library ingestion, required AI, hidden source
  synthesis, or publication action
- **Concerns:** Intention, Attention, Continuity, Authority, Boundaries, Learning
- **Moves used:** Orient, Capture, Clarify, Connect, Choose, Continue, Reflect

## Situation

An individual is developing a long-form essay over several months. Early ideas
live in three paper notebooks. Selected research notes and source excerpts live
in ordinary digital files. The manuscript file is the record of authority for
accepted prose. The initiative pauses twice for several weeks.

The challenge is not capturing everything. It is retaining enough truthful
context to return without confusing sketches, source claims, AI proposals, and
accepted manuscript decisions.

## Establish authority by purpose

The individual records:

| Purpose | Record of authority |
| --- | --- |
| Original ideation and field observations | The specific paper notebook page |
| Claims from research material | The selected digital source note linked to its source |
| Accepted structure and prose | The manuscript file |
| Current return point | A replaceable continuity cue |
| Private learning about the practice | Reflection on its chosen surface; Journal only if deliberately admitted |

No single system controls the entire initiative.

## First paper-to-digital selection

During ideation, the individual marks twelve notebook pages but admits only two
passages into digital practice: one central question and one structural sketch.
Each captured image is linked to its page. Literal derivatives preserve arrows,
cross-outs, and one uncertain word.

The individual corrects the uncertain word, then promotes a cleaned structural
outline into the manuscript. The source image and reviewed derivative remain
distinct. The unselected notebook pages remain physical.

## Digital research and source-backed assistance

The individual creates bounded source notes for six research items. Each note
separates direct claims, selected excerpts, personal interpretation, and open
questions.

They optionally admit three source notes and the current outline to AI for one
purpose: propose where claims conflict and identify missing support. The
assistance must cite the supporting note and cannot read the notebooks, full
source library, Journal, or unrelated files.

The assistance proposes five issues:

- two source conflicts are supported and accepted as review questions;
- one supposed conflict comes from an outdated projection and is rejected;
- one missing citation is confirmed by the individual; and
- one proposed conclusion exceeds the sources and remains rejected.

No proposal enters the manuscript automatically.

## Interruption and return

Before the first long pause, the individual leaves a continuity cue:

> Why: test whether the central claim survives the two conflicts. State: outline
> accepted; section two is provisional. Next: inspect source notes B and D
> directly before revising. Stop if the supporting claim remains unresolved.

On return, one source note has been corrected. The individual treats the cue as
stale context, compares it with the authoritative source note, corrects the
provisional section, and records a new cue.

Before the second pause, they return a minimal prompt to paper:

> Sketch a version of the argument that does not depend on the unresolved claim.

The later handwritten response is a new source. It is selectively captured,
reviewed, and used to change the manuscript structure.

## Choose and continue

The individual repeatedly chooses among:

- continuing a supported section;
- deferring a claim that needs more evidence;
- releasing an attractive but unsupported idea;
- leaving a paper passage un-digitized;
- rejecting an AI proposal; and
- stopping when review burden exceeds the value of another source.

The manuscript changes only through the individual's accepted edits. Publishing
the essay is a separate external action outside the case.

## Reflect

At the end of the draft, the individual observes that the return cues were most
useful when they named an unresolved decision, not just a next action. They also
note that capturing more notebook pages would have increased correction burden
without changing the manuscript.

The individual deliberately admits the first observation as a private Journal
Entry and leaves the second as a margin note. Neither becomes a universal rule.

## Provenance and authority

- Notebook pages remain physical sources.
- Captures, literal derivatives, and corrected derivatives retain separate
  identities.
- Source notes control the research claims they record.
- The manuscript controls accepted structure and prose.
- AI output remains source-backed proposals.
- Continuity cues are replaceable projections whose freshness is checked on
  return.
- The later paper response is a new linked source.

## Boundaries

- Only material needed for a current question is admitted.
- Assistance cannot expand into the full research library.
- The individual checks consequential claims against sources.
- No tool publishes, shares, or changes an external record.
- Provider exit leaves notebooks, source notes, manuscript, corrections, and
  links usable through ordinary forms.
- The initiative may stop without exhausting every source or idea.

## Illustrative outcome

The initiative survives interruptions and surface changes while preserving
authority and source support. Several ideas are released rather than captured
or completed. This case does not prove Focus improves creative quality or that
the illustrated research practice fits every project.

## Lessons

- Long-running continuity benefits from naming unresolved decisions.
- Source-backed assistance can reveal review questions without owning the
  manuscript.
- Selective capture protects both burden and provenance.
- A new physical response can materially redirect digital work without making
  paper subordinate.
- Reflection should improve the local practice without becoming a productivity
  measure.
