# 03: Physical-digital-physical round trip

- **Status:** Fictional, illustrative, and non-authoritative
- **Purpose:** Carry selected garden-layout thinking from paper into a reviewed
  digital plan and back to a site notebook
- **Life scope:** Personal
- **Assumptions:** The individual wants retrieval and comparison for one selected
  sketch but wants the notebook to remain primary for field observations
- **Limits:** No whole-notebook ingestion, required AI, ambient capture, or
  destruction of paper
- **Concerns:** Intention, Continuity, Authority, Boundaries, Learning
- **Moves used:** Orient, Capture, Clarify, Connect, Choose, Continue, Reflect

## Situation

An individual sketches a small garden layout in a paper notebook during a site
walk. The sketch includes dimensions, sun observations, and two alternative
layouts. Later, they want to compare the dimensions with a digital planning
document while keeping the notebook as the record of authority for field
observations.

## Round trip

### Orient

The purpose is limited: compare one layout with existing measurements and carry
a useful prompt back to the next site walk. The individual excludes adjacent
pages and unrelated household notes.

### Capture

They photograph only the selected page. The capture record identifies the
notebook, page mark, capture date, and purpose. The image is temporary until the
literal derivative is reviewed.

### Clarify

A literal derivative preserves the written dimensions and marks one value as
uncertain. The individual compares it with the paper source, corrects a mistaken
digit, and records the correction without altering the image or initial
derivative.

An optional assistance could have produced the literal derivative, but it would
remain a proposal until this human comparison. The example does not require AI.

### Connect

The individual relates the reviewed dimensions to the existing digital planning
document. That document is designated as the record of authority for the current
layout decision; the paper notebook remains authoritative for field
observations.

They promote only the corrected dimensions and a pointer to the selected page.
The alternatives and surrounding notebook content remain private and physical.

### Choose

The comparison rules out one layout. The individual accepts the other as the
current digital plan but leaves the uncertain sun observation unresolved until
another site walk.

### Continue

They write a minimal physical prompt on a new notebook page:

> Recheck late-day sun at the north edge. Current layout decision is in the
> digital plan; do not redraw unless the observation changes it.

At the next site walk, the individual records a new observation below that
prompt. This is a new linked physical source, not an edit to the original sketch.

### Reflect

The individual notes that retaining the full captured image is unnecessary once
the derivative and source link are verified. They delete the provider copy under
the chosen policy and keep the physical source.

## Provenance and authority

| Artifact | Status | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original notebook sketch | Physical source | Record of authority for field observations |
| Selected photograph | Captured-image derivative | Temporary review aid |
| Initial transcription | Literal derivative | Non-authoritative; uncertainty visible |
| Corrected dimensions | Human-reviewed derivative | Eligible for bounded promotion |
| Digital planning document | Accepted digital record | Record of authority for the current layout decision |
| New notebook prompt | Physical continuity cue | Points to the digital decision and unresolved observation |
| Later site observation | New linked physical source | Controls the new field observation |

## Boundaries

- Only one page is admitted.
- The source is retained and remains findable without the capture provider.
- The captured image, literal derivative, corrected text, accepted record, and
  later physical source remain distinct.
- One record does not control every purpose.
- An uncertain observation is not resolved by a cleaned derivative.
- Provider deletion is recorded only when visibly completed; it is not inferred.

## Illustrative outcome

The selected idea moves from paper to a reviewed digital decision and returns as
a minimal physical prompt that produces a new source. This illustrates the
[physical and digital practice](../framework/physical-digital-practice.md); it is
not a required Focus lifecycle or evidence that round trips are always worth the
burden.

## Lessons

- Digitization should have a stated purpose and narrow selection.
- Records of authority can differ by purpose.
- Human correction does not erase the original derivative.
- A physical prompt can point to digital context without duplicating it.
- A later handwritten response is a new source with its own provenance.
