# 05: Personal Mission Control across a mixed week

- **Status:** Fictional, illustrative, and non-authoritative complex case study
- **Purpose:** Direct attention across selected work and personal contexts
  during a crowded week
- **Life scope:** Combined, with separate work and personal boundaries
- **Assumptions:** Existing systems remain authoritative; Personal Mission
  Control is a replaceable derived view
- **Limits:** No whole-life ingestion, team access, automatic priority, blanket
  Journal access, or autonomous external action
- **Concerns:** Intention, Attention, Continuity, Authority, Boundaries, Learning
- **Moves used:** Orient, Capture, Clarify, Connect, Choose, Continue, Reflect

## Situation

An individual has three selected contexts:

- a work proposal that needs a decision by Thursday;
- a personal home-service appointment on Wednesday; and
- a private Journal Entry about protecting recovery time after a demanding
  event.

The work document, personal calendar, and private Journal remain separate
records of authority. The individual chooses to compose a temporary Personal
Mission Control view for this week.

## Orient and selectively admit

The individual states the current intention: meet the real commitments while
preserving enough recovery capacity to make sound decisions. They admit:

- the work proposal's current decision point and due date, not the full work
  history;
- the appointment time and preparation need, not unrelated calendar events;
- one sentence from the selected Journal Entry, not the rest of the Journal;
- two recent Transition Check-Ins; and
- a projection of the shared work record's status.

The view excludes personal messages, other Journal Entries, the complete work
system, and unattended sources.

## Clarify authority and freshness

The Mission Control view shows:

| Matter | Record of authority | Projection freshness | Why now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work proposal decision | Shared work document | Observed Tuesday morning | Review is needed before Thursday |
| Home appointment | Personal calendar | Observed Tuesday morning | Preparation must happen before Wednesday |
| Recovery boundary | Private Journal Entry | Entry selected Tuesday | It affects available capacity this week |

One Check-In says the proposal is ready for approval, but the shared work
document still shows an unresolved amount. The conflict remains visible. The
view does not choose the newer timestamp or silently merge them.

## Bounded Focus Review

The individual limits the review to twenty minutes and four Review Items:

1. confirm the work amount from the authoritative source;
2. choose whether to prepare for the home appointment now or Wednesday morning;
3. decide what work can be deferred to protect the selected recovery boundary;
4. dismiss or retain an AI-proposed continuity summary.

Each item shows its source, freshness, why now, choices, and authority. The
individual corrects the stale Check-In, chooses appointment preparation now,
defers a nonessential work item, and dismisses one reminder as redundant.

## Optional AI proposals

The individual admits the four Review Items and their visible sources to AI for
one purpose: propose a two-sentence orientation summary and identify any
unverified consequential detail.

The proposal correctly flags the unresolved work amount and suggests that the
personal appointment is flexible. The source does not support flexibility, so
the individual rejects that suggestion. They accept a corrected orientation
summary as a temporary projection, not an authoritative record.

AI does not receive the full Journal Entry or external accounts. It cannot edit
the work document, calendar, or Journal.

## Bounded shared promotion

After verifying the work amount, the individual prepares a short decision note
for the shared work document. The preview contains:

- the confirmed amount;
- the decision and rationale needed by the shared record; and
- no personal appointment, Journal, recovery context, private Check-In, or AI
  history.

The individual performs the update in the authoritative work system. An Action
Receipt records the exact destination, requested change, successful result, and
destination readback. The private practice remains private.

## Continue and Reflect

The individual completes the home preparation, pauses the deferred work, and
leaves a new continuity cue for the proposal. At the end of the week, they
Reflect that the four-item bound was useful but the redundant reminder added
burden. They remove that projection from the next review.

The Reflection stays in a temporary note; it is not automatically added to the
Journal.

## Provenance and authority

- The shared work document controls the proposal decision and amount.
- The personal calendar controls the appointment.
- The designated Journal controls the selected Journal Entry.
- Check-Ins, AI summaries, and Mission Control are temporary projections or
  proposals.
- The Action Receipt proves the attempted shared mutation and its readback; it
  does not make Mission Control authoritative.

## Boundaries

- Combined life scope is selective, not comprehensive.
- Work and personal records remain separate.
- Journal resurfacing is limited to one selected sentence.
- The view never gains team or manager access.
- AI output cannot approve or execute the shared promotion.
- Deferral, dismissal, and no action remain available.
- The view can be discarded without losing authoritative records.

## Illustrative outcome

The individual resolves one conflict, makes a mixed-life attention choice,
promotes one bounded work artifact, and removes a burdensome reminder. The case
shows one possible Personal Mission Control practice. It does not specify an
application or prove that a composed view is better than separate notes.

## Lessons

- Mission Control should compose authority, not absorb it.
- Why now and freshness expose stale or unsupported suggestions.
- Combined scope can support choice without merging private and shared records.
- AI proposals may be partly useful while remaining unaccepted.
- A shared promotion must exclude originating private context and produce an
  Action Receipt.
