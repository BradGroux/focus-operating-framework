# 01: Paper-only personal reset

- **Status:** Fictional, illustrative, and non-authoritative
- **Purpose:** Resume a paused household repair plan after an interruption
- **Life scope:** Personal
- **Assumptions:** The individual has the original paper plan and needs no shared
  update
- **Limits:** No digitization, AI, connector, Journal, or permanent Check-In
- **Concerns:** Intention, Attention, Continuity, Authority, Boundaries, Learning
- **Moves used:** Orient, Choose, Continue, Reflect

## Situation

An individual spent part of a weekend planning a small household repair on a
sheet of paper. An unexpected obligation interrupted the work. Two days later,
they return with less time and different energy than expected.

The paper already contains the measurements, material list, and a rough order
of work. It is the source and the individual's record of authority for this
private plan.

## Practice

### Orient

The individual reads the existing sheet and adds a short Transition Check-In:

> Now: the plan is sound, but I have only thirty minutes. Next chosen step:
> verify the first measurement and prepare the work area. Stop if the measured
> condition differs from the sketch.

This records present context, the next chosen step, and a valid stopping point.
It does not create a task list or claim the full repair must be completed.

### Choose

The original intention was to finish the repair. The individual revises the
current choice to preparation only. They deliberately defer buying materials
and release the expectation of completion that day.

### Continue

They verify the first measurement, find that it differs from the sketch, and
stop as planned. They cross out the earlier value without making it unreadable,
write the corrected measurement beside it, and add the reason for the
correction.

### Reflect

In the margin, they note that the stopping condition prevented an unnecessary
purchase. The Reflection remains on the same sheet. It is not promoted into a
Journal.

## Provenance and authority

| Artifact | Status | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original repair sheet | Physical source | Record of authority for the private plan |
| Transition Check-In | Ephemeral physical note | Continuity cue only |
| Corrected measurement | Human correction linked to the source | Accepted on the physical plan for this purpose |
| Margin Reflection | Physical Reflection | Remains on its original surface |

## Boundaries

- Nothing is digitized because retrieval, sharing, and cross-surface connection
  are unnecessary.
- The Check-In can be erased or ignored after the next return.
- The person changes the next step rather than obeying the original completion
  expectation.
- No score, streak, reminder, or external record is created.

## Illustrative outcome

The individual stops with a corrected source and a clear next return point. The
episode demonstrates Focus continuity without digital capture. It does not show
that paper is always preferable or that the practice will reduce restart effort
for others.

## Lessons

- Paper-only is a complete path.
- A Transition Check-In can be minimal and temporary.
- Stopping can be the responsible Continue outcome.
- Correction should preserve the earlier source value and the reason for change.
