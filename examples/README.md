# Examples

These seven examples are original, fictional, tool-neutral, and
non-authoritative. They illustrate ways an individual might apply Focus; they
do not define a required workflow, application, data model, or proof of
effectiveness.

Read the [charter](../framework/charter.md),
[concerns and moves](../framework/concerns-and-moves.md), and
[practice guide](../framework/practice-guide.md) before treating an example as
guidance.

## Focused practice examples

1. [Paper-only personal reset](01-paper-only-personal-reset.md) — a minimal
   Transition Check-In and chosen next step with no digitization.
2. [Digital-only work resumption](02-digital-only-work-resumption.md) —
   continuity through an interruption without creating another task system.
3. [Physical-digital-physical round trip](03-physical-digital-physical-round-trip.md)
   — selective capture, review, promotion, and a new linked physical response.
4. [Private Journal Reflection with optional AI](04-private-journal-reflection-with-optional-ai.md)
   — one deliberately selected Journal Entry, bounded assistance, and no shared
   promotion.

## Complex case studies

5. [Personal Mission Control across a mixed week](05-personal-mission-control-across-a-mixed-week.md)
   — selected work and personal context, Review Items, AI proposals, shared
   promotion, and an Action Receipt.
6. [Long-running creative research initiative](06-long-running-creative-research-initiative.md)
   — paper ideation, digital sources, source-backed assistance, interruption,
   authoritative decisions, physical prompts, and Reflection.
7. [Disruption and recovery](07-disruption-and-recovery.md) — stale projections,
   conflicts, competing work and personal changes, manual continuity, provider
   exit, and a reflective reset.

## How to read an example

Each example states:

- purpose and life scope;
- assumptions and limits;
- applicable concerns and moves;
- artifacts, provenance, and records of authority;
- boundaries and non-action choices;
- illustrative outcome; and
- lessons that remain subordinate to the canonical framework.

An implementation may realize the same example differently. A practitioner may
use fewer moves, another surface, no AI, no Journal, no Mission Control, or no
durable record at all.
