# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Participation in the Focus Operating Framework should make the work clearer,
safer, and more useful. Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and practitioners
are expected to treat one another as people with agency, boundaries, and
different contexts.

## Expected conduct

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Critique ideas, wording, evidence, and consequences rather than people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual boundaries.
- Make room for disagreement, correction, deferral, and withdrawal.
- Keep public discussion free of personal records, confidential material,
  credentials, private Journal content, and sensitive third-party information.
- Surface possible harm, coercion, surveillance, authority drift, or
  accessibility barriers early.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to narrow, pause, or close a discussion when
  the public venue is unsafe or the proposal exceeds repository scope.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or demeaning language.
- Publishing or soliciting private or identifying information without clear
  authority.
- Using a contribution process to pressure, score, monitor, or manipulate a
  person.
- Repeated unwanted contact after a boundary has been stated.
- Presenting fabricated evidence, concealed conflicts, or private source
  history as public review proof.
- Using access to the repository to execute unrelated external actions.

## Reporting and response

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue or pull request. Use
the private reporting path in the [security and privacy policy](SECURITY.md).
Include only the minimum information needed to understand and respond to the
concern.

The steward may remove content, restrict participation, request correction,
pause a change, or take another proportionate action. A response should protect
affected people, preserve necessary evidence privately, avoid unnecessary
disclosure, and document public repository consequences only when appropriate.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository spaces and to public conduct undertaken as a
representative of the project. It does not create authority over a person's
unrelated private life.
